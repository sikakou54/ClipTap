FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How much can I do for free?

Free includes unlimited templates and categories, unlimited keyboard items, and all 7 system variables with all 94 formats. You can also use up to 3 profiles and 5 custom variables for free.

Where is my data stored?

Templates, categories, profiles and variables are all stored on your device. They are not stored in the cloud, and the contents of your templates are not sent outside your device.

Does ClipTap work offline?

Creating, searching, expanding variables, copying and keyboard input all work offline. Only account linking, Pro entitlement checks, ads and store navigation need a network connection.

Does the iPhone need any setup?

You need to add the ClipTap keyboard in Settings first. Full Access is needed to record usage counts, but inserting templates works without Full Access.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Export a .cliptap file and import it on the new device. ClipTap performs a password-match check and checksum verification, but the file contents are not encrypted.

What happens when I cancel Pro?

Pro remains available until the end of the paid period. Profiles and custom variables above the Free limits are not deleted; they are only disabled and become available again if you return to Pro.

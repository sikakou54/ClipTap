What is ClipTap

ClipTap is a text template app for the phrases you retype every day. The ClipTap keyboard extension inserts them straight into Gmail, Slack, LINE, X, Instagram, ChatGPT, your browser or any other app – no app-switching, no copy-and-paste.

Email greetings, address and phone number, quotes and invoices, hashtags and bios, AI prompts: anything you keep retyping becomes a reusable template. Variables fill in the parts that change – {{today}} and {{now}} for the date and time, custom variables like {{name}} or {{company}} for your own data.

Who is ClipTap for?

• Business professionals who send similar emails and chats and switch signatures between roles
• Daily SNS users who reuse the same captions, hashtag sets and bios
• Freelancers sending quotes, invoices and reminders who keep client data on their device
• Anyone building a prompt library for ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

Main features

• Keyboard extension
Tap a template in the app to copy it, or tap it on the ClipTap keyboard to insert it into any app – email, chat, SNS or your browser.

• Variables for the parts that change
The 7 system variables ({{today}}, {{now}}, {{time}}, {{year}}, {{month}}, {{day}}, {{weekday}}) are free for everyone. Custom variables ({{name}}, {{email}}, {{company}}, {{client}}) hold your own data: register once, reuse everywhere.
Free: 5 custom variables / Pro: unlimited.

• Date and time formats you choose (new, free)
Pick an output format for each system variable from 94 presets: 2026/08/04, 08/04/2026, 04/08/2026, 09:05, Tue, (Tue), Tuesday. Preview it with the real date, and every template using that variable follows at once – keyboard included.

• A prompt library for AI chats
Keep the prompts you reuse – summarize, translate, rewrite, review – as templates. Add variables so only the date or project name changes, and insert them into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini from the keyboard.

• Environments (profiles)
Profiles like “Company A”, “Side business” and “Personal”: switch profile and the same templates come out with different company names, titles and signatures.
Free: 3 profiles / Pro: unlimited.

• Web version (PC browser)
Organize a large library on a big screen and move data between phone and browser as a .cliptap file. New here? Download the starter file from “Getting started” and begin right away, even without the mobile app. “Close file” clears the browser's working data.

• Categories & search
Group templates into categories such as “Work”, “SNS”, “Prompts” and “Code”, and find them fast with keyword search.

• Import / export
Export to a file and import it on another device. Handy when changing phones, or when you want the same setup on a tablet and the web version.

Security & privacy

Templates and variable data stay on your device. No cloud sync, no automatic upload – apart from subscription processing, sign-in and ads. Core features work offline, so client data, confidential content and personal information stay with you.

Free and Pro

Free: unlimited templates and categories, the keyboard extension, all 7 system variables and every format preset, 3 profiles, 5 custom variables.
Pro (monthly or yearly): no ads, unlimited profiles and custom variables, plus everything in Free. Billing runs through your app store account; cancel any time and Pro stays until the paid period ends.
